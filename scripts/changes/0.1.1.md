## What changed in 0.1.1

**Lock-screen player**
- Two styles: **Card** (wide) and **Artwork** (square, the artwork fills the card). Pick one in Settings → Pulau → Lock screen.
- **Liquid Glass** frame, title chip and buttons for the Artwork style on macOS 26+ (toggle in Settings).
- The current lyric line lights up **word by word** (timing is estimated unless the lyrics include word timestamps).
- **Click or drag the progress bar** to seek.
- The card is **tinted by the artwork colour**.
- The card **hides after a long pause** (1 / 5 / 15 minutes or never; default 5) instead of a few seconds after pausing.
- **Favourite (heart) button** in the Artwork style for Apple Music and YouTube Music.

**Lyrics**
- Word-timestamp tags in enhanced LRC lyrics are no longer shown as raw text in the Lyrics tab.
